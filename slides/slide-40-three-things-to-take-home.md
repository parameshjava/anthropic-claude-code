# Slide 40: Three things to take home

### The operating model boils down to three habits

### 1. Choose reasoning deliberately

Match model and effort to uncertainty, risk, and change surface. Do not spend the strongest reasoning on a typo, and do not expect a low-effort local edit to resolve an ambiguous cross-layer design.

### 2. Keep context intentional

Start from known files and durable project guidance. Load procedures, integrations, and broad research only when the task needs them. Compact the same task; clear unrelated work.

### 3. Attach proof before completion

Define the test, build, screenshot, contract check, or review that proves the change. A completion message is a claim; a runnable result and inspectable diff are evidence.

### One practical habit

Before starting: choose the model, effort, scope, and proof. Before finishing: inspect the evidence, final diff, and remaining risk. That is the operating model that scales.
